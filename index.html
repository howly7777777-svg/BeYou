Welcome

Be You Want to Be is a local-first daily practice for iOS. Write one “I am” sentence small enough to already be true today, read it in the morning, and note one piece of evidence at night. An optional moment—“When…, I…”—can sit in the midday reminder and on the Home Screen widget.

This is a daily practice, not treatment or a diagnosis. It does not promise outcomes. Your sentence, your evidence, and your settings stay on your device.

Local-First Data

Your sentence, optional moment, evening notes, and practice history are stored on your device. No account is required. Reminders are local notifications, not remote push. If your device can suggest a smaller sentence, that suggestion is made on the device. Otherwise the App uses a built-in line. We do not receive the text. Optional iCloud sync—if you turn it on—stores data in your private iCloud, linked to your Apple ID. We cannot read it.
